Who it is for

  • A small business in Ireland with one location: a shop, a trade, a studio, a salon, a clinic, or similar.
  • An owner who can say, in their own words, what they do, where they are, when they are open, and which services they actually offer.
  • Someone who will paste the pack into Google themselves, or who will add a manager if they want the fields entered for them.

Who it is not for

  • A second premises. One payment is one location. Another address is another pack.
  • A business that wants a rank, a map-pack place, or a promise of more calls. Google says there is no way to request or pay for a better local ranking.
  • Anyone who wants fake reviews, paid reviews, or a review written for a customer to paste.
  • Ads, a website build, invoice chasing, or a monthly posting retainer. Those are not in the €149.
  • A listing Profile Ready would have to invent. If you cannot say what you do, there is nothing accurate to write.

Exactly what you get

A paste-ready pack for one location, using only facts you supply:

  1. A suggested primary category, named to match the work you actually do, with a short reason. Extra categories only where you really offer that work.
  2. A business description of 750 characters or fewer.
  3. A services list: the name of each service and a short factual line.
  4. An hours block. A day you did not specify is marked missing, not guessed.
  5. Two short Google posts, ready to paste. No offer or discount unless you wrote it in the intake.
  6. A review-request email you send yourself to people who were actually customers, plus a one-line reply you can paste under a new review. Profile Ready does not send that email.

Google Business Profile itself is free to create. The €149 is for the finished pack. If you later add Kenneth as a manager, the same fields can be entered on the live profile. Do not share your Google password. If you will not grant access, you still get the pack to paste yourself. Google decides verification. A postcard or a video check stays with you. Approval is not promised.

Worked example

Example only · not a customer · not a live listing

Paper Lantern Clocks is a fictional one-person clock-repair bench in Wexford town. The owner, Sam Doyle, is also fictional. Sam’s intake said: repairs to mechanical clocks and watches, open Tuesday to Friday 10:00–17:00, Saturday 10:00–14:00, closed Sunday and Monday, phone 053 000 0000, no website. Those hours and that phone number are made up.

The pack would include, in substance:

  • Primary category: something that matches clock and watch repair, with a one-line reason tied to the work Sam named. Not a rank claim.
  • A description under 750 characters, for example: “Paper Lantern Clocks repairs mechanical clocks and watches in Wexford town. The bench is open Tuesday to Friday, 10:00–17:00, and Saturday, 10:00–14:00. Closed Sunday and Monday. Call 053 000 0000 to ask about a repair.” The real pack would show the character count and stay at or under 750.
  • Services: mechanical clock repair; watch repair. No service Sam did not name.
  • The hours above, with Sunday and Monday marked Closed.
  • Two posts that repeat the town, the two services, and the hours. No discount.
  • A review email Sam would send, only to people who had actually left a clock, with no reward for reviewing. Profile Ready would not send it.

The live profile would be edited only if Sam granted manager access. Google would still decide verification. Nothing in this example was published.
